LEARNING JOURNEY

Four Dimensions of Growth at Dewford

A learning journey shaped around each child’s development.
Admissions Inquiry

Confidence

01

The confidence to express ideas with clarity.

Children develop the confidence to put their thoughts into words, share what they discover, and communicate their ideas with conviction.

Curiosity

02

The Curiosity to Explore and Discover

Children learn to turn everyday questions into opportunities for exploration, making connections and discovering new ideas through inquiry and hands-on learning.

Communication

03

The Ability to Connect and Communicate

Children learn to listen, share their ideas, and appreciate different perspectives through conversations and collaboration.

Independence

04

The Ability to Take Ownership of Learning

Children learn to set their own goals, take responsibility for their progress, and approach new challenges with confidence and initiative.
