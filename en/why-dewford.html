Why DEWFORD

A Child Who Thinks,

and Expresses with Confidence.

At Dewford, children go beyond learning English.
They learn to think critically, make connections, and express their ideas with confidence.

We provide a learning environment where children build a strong foundation in English while developing the ability to think independently and communicate their ideas clearly.
Learning Through Experience
Children learn by exploring, questioning, and making discoveries. Through hands-on experiences, they connect new ideas with what they already know and develop a deeper understanding of the world.
Learning in Everyday Life
Learning extends beyond the classroom. By connecting everyday experiences with learning opportunities, children develop practical skills, deepen their understanding, and discover how learning relates to the world around them.
Learning Through Interaction
Through conversations and collaboration, children share perspectives, build relationships, and learn from one another. These interactions strengthen communication skills, encourage independent thinking, and foster a sense of community.
THE DEWFORD DIFFERENCE

Five Commitments

Small Class Environment

Small classes create more opportunities for meaningful interaction, allowing teachers to understand each child’s needs and provide attentive, individualized support.

Individual Learning Program

Every child learns differently. We consider each child’s learning level, needs, and progress to provide appropriate instruction and support individual growth.

Literacy & Reading Pathway

A structured approach to literacy develops vocabulary and reading comprehension. Children learn to understand and interpret texts, connect ideas, and build the skills needed to become independent readers.

Inquiry & Project Experience

Through inquiry and hands-on projects across a range of topics, children explore ideas, investigate questions, and solve problems. They learn to think critically, make connections, and communicate their ideas clearly.

Home Learning & Partnership

We share insights into each child’s learning and development with families. Ongoing communication between home and school helps create a consistent and supportive environment for growth.
LEARNING JOURNEY

Every Child Has a Unique Starting Point.

Every child learns and grows in their own way. We take the time to understand each child’s abilities, interests, and learning needs to help identify the right learning path.
